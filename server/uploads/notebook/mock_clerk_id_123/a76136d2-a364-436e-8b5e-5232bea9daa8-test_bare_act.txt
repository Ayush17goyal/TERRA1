Indian Penal Code Notes
The term Theft refers to the dishonest removal of moveable property.
The word Murder means the intentional causing of death of a human being.
Under Section 378 of the Indian Penal Code, theft is defined as taking moveable property without consent.
Under Section 300, murder is defined as intentional homicide.
According to Article 21 of the Constitution of India, no person shall be deprived of life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.
In the landmark case of Keshvananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973), the Supreme Court of India held that the basic structure of the Constitution cannot be amended.
In the famous case of Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India (1978), the court held that the right to travel abroad is part of personal liberty under Article 21.
Statutory rules must comply with constitutional parameters.
